About Me

Bridging Research, Education & Public Health Practice

I work at the intersection of academia, research, and frontline healthcare — committed to reducing health inequalities and improving emergency care access for underserved communities.

As a Fellow of the Royal Society for Public Health (FRSPH), I bring a rigorous evidence-based lens to every project. As Principal Investigator at the Yorkshire Ambulance Service (YAS) Research Institute I led a study of how deprivation affects access to emergency and urgent care in Yorkshire, funded by an NIHR Research Capability Funding (RCF) award issued by the Trust. From October 2026 I take up an Alzheimer’s Society-funded doctoral studentship at the Centre for Dementia Research, Leeds Beckett University, studying how people living with dementia are supported to manage comorbid long-term conditions. Alongside this I lecture in Health and Care Management at Arden University and work as a Peer Researcher at Leeds Beckett University.
